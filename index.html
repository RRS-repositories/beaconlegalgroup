Areas of Work

Claim types our partner firms handle

Our support team is trained in the case types below, so the law firms we work with can hand over volume work with confidence. These are the areas we support — the claims themselves are always conducted by the instructing firm.

Car Finance Claims

Document review, agreement analysis and file preparation support for firms running motor finance affordability and commission caseloads.

Store Card Claims

Data sorting and evidence bundling for store card affordability complaints, ready for the instructing firm's review and submission.

Overdraft Misuse Claims

Statement analysis and long-term overdraft reliance mapping to support the firm's assessment of persistent debt cases.

Credit Card Claims

Limit-increase timelines, spend analysis and chronology preparation for credit card irresponsible lending files.

Personal Loan Claims

Income and expenditure workups, repeat-lending pattern analysis and draft document preparation for unaffordable loan cases.

Packaged Bank Account Claims

Fee reconciliation and benefit-usage review to help firms assess and progress packaged account mis-sale complaints.

What We Provide

Outsourced support built for volume casework

Beacon Legal Group is a Manchester-based outsourcing organisation. We work behind the scenes for law firms whose caseloads have outgrown their in-house capacity — providing trained people, structured processes and reliable turnaround, without the overhead of recruiting.

Every file we touch remains under the control, supervision and professional responsibility of the instructing firm. We provide the hands; your firm provides the legal judgement.

Overflow, not takeover We never act for claimants and we never give legal advice. Our role is capacity: your cases stay your cases.

Document & data processing

DSAR review, statement analysis, evidence sorting and structured data extraction, delivered in your firm's preferred format.

File preparation

Chronologies, affordability workups and draft correspondence prepared for review and sign-off by the instructing firm's fee earners.

Confidential & secure

Strict confidentiality agreements, secure data handling and UK GDPR-compliant processes on every engagement.
